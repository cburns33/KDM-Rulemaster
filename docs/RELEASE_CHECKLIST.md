# First-release checklist: opening White Lion and early campaign

Defined 24 October 2026 following the user's instruction to focus the first release on opening White Lion and early campaign play. This checklist owns the release boundary and acceptance requirements. [Status](STATUS.md) owns present progress; [coverage](COVERAGE.md) lists existing reviewed evidence.

## Release promise and boundary

Help the group play the Prologue, resolve the first settlement, undertake the first level 1 White Lion hunt/showdown, and resolve its returning-survivor settlement. Support the shared mechanics needed to repeat that early White Lion loop. This is a bounded first release, not complete core-campaign coverage.

The assistant must explain covered mechanics and card interactions from reviewable 1.5 evidence, show the sources, ask for missing context, and state missing coverage. A required topic that currently lacks evidence remains a release blocker even if the app declines it well.

The first-settlement and level 1 boundary is the working interpretation of early campaign for this release. It does not include later story milestones, other monsters, expansions, or compatibility with another edition. Cunning is excluded from both required coverage and the evaluation questions.

## Explicit exclusions and contextual coverage

- Cunning: preserve the unverified gap; do not acquire, test, or guess its effect in this release work.
- Other monsters, expansions, later Age milestones, later campaign story content, and edition 1.6 certification: outside the first release.
- Complete random hunt/settlement event, innovation, gear, fighting-art, disorder, and resource-card libraries: outside this first release. The phase procedures and fixed starting/initial-settlement content above remain required.
- A specific excluded card or event may be answered only if its exact evidence has been reviewed. Otherwise request the missing card/text or state the limit. A user-provided source needs review before the app claims a supported interpretation.
- Complete level 2/3 White Lion behavior is excluded. Do not apply level 1 assumptions to a question that changes monster level.

## Answer acceptance checklist

- [ ] A covered question receives applicable evidence and a materially correct explanation, with conditions and exceptions that affect the result.
- [ ] Combined card/rule questions preserve the actual target conditions, triggers, and persistent-injury overrides. Nearby records do not substitute for missing card text.
- [ ] Missing context triggers a useful clarification. Follow-ups retain relevant facts and replace facts the user changes.
- [ ] Required missing sources are reported as coverage blockers. Excluded subjects receive an appropriate scoped response without invented effects, stats, or table outcomes.
- [ ] Every source citation opens the correct image/page with the correct revised/printed mapping. Provenance and edition claims stay within the evidence.
- [ ] Sol uses only reviewed selected evidence; local tables and boundary handling retain their intended behavior. A missing/disabled model is recorded as untested, not a model pass.

Report all case counts, failures, and exclusions instead of treating this small sample as an accuracy guarantee.
