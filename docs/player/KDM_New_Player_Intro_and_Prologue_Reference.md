# Kingdom Death: Monster — first session

## Getting started

We’re starting a shared campaign with four survivors. First we’ll play the opening White Lion showdown (the Prologue) and make our first settlement decisions. After that, the campaign moves through Hunts, Showdowns, and Settlement phases.

The Prologue teaches the rules as we play. **No rulebook reading or video is required beforehand.** Read this page once; keep the bottom half handy during the fight. We’ll explain new rules when they become relevant.

## Comparisons, by category

| Game | Comparison category | What carries over |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Gloomhaven** | Showdown tactics | Talk through positioning and attacks together. |
| **Frosthaven** | Campaign structure | What happens on a mission changes what the shared home base can do. |
| **Monster Hunter: World** | Hunt and gear progression | Learn a monster; use what you get from it to prepare for later fights. |
| **XCOM 2** | Tactical risk | A good plan still has uncertain rolls, and losses can affect later outings. |
| **Fire Emblem** | Permanent loss | You may lose a survivor you’ve become attached to. |
| **Darkest Dungeon** | Overall tone | A fragile roster takes dangerous trips and returns to develop a home base. No familiarity with it needed. |

These are comparisons, not rules: we’ll use KDM’s cards and the Prologue to learn how this game actually works.

## What you control

Each new player controls **one survivor** and decides what that survivor does. The person handling the physical game will move pieces and show the cards. The group can discuss decisions, but you make the call for your survivor. You’ll have a survivor sheet, a gear grid, and two starting gear cards: **Founding Stone** and **Cloth**. The monster’s AI and Hit Location cards are shared; we’ll reveal and read them as they come up.

## Keep this open during the Prologue

### Your turn

1. Move up to your **Movement** value and take **one activation**, in either order. An activation can be an attack or another action permitted by a card or rule.
2. To attack, roll a number of dice equal to your weapon’s **Speed**. Rolls that meet its **Accuracy**, after modifiers, hit.
3. Draw **one monster Hit Location card per hit**. Choose the order and resolve them one at a time.
4. For each wound attempt, roll a d10 and add your **Strength** and weapon **Strength**. Compare the total with the monster’s **Toughness**.
5. Read and resolve any reaction at the time the card specifies. A **Trap** interrupts the attack: stop resolving the other Hit Locations and follow its card.

### Monster turn

1. Draw an **AI card** and follow its target instructions literally.
2. Move the monster as the card directs and roll its attack.
3. Roll the **survivor Hit Location die** for each successful hit, then apply armor, damage, and injuries as instructed.

You start with **1 Survival** from naming your survivor. During the Prologue, you can spend it to **Dodge** a hit when the rules allow. Other Survival actions become available later.

**READ CARDS LITERALLY. POSITIONING MATTERS. DON’T ASSUME YOU NEED TO ATTACK EVERY TURN. DEATH IS PART OF THE GAME.**

For exact numbers, check your Founding Stone and Cloth cards, gear grid, and survivor sheet. Ask to see a card again before deciding.

After the fight, we’ll go through the first Settlement together. There’s nothing to study for that phase.
