## S03

UNREVIEWED WIKI DRAFT | Edition 1.5 unverified

Card: Combo Claw
Question: What is Combo Claw's attack profile?

**Combo Claw** picks a target in this order:

1. The closest threat that is facing and in range.
2. The closest threat in its field of view.
3. If there is no target, **sniff**.

It then moves and attacks the target with **Speed 2, Accuracy 4+, Damage 1**. If this attack deals damage more than once, draw an AI card.

The draft does not define **sniff** or the linked targeting terms, so their exact effects cannot be determined from it.
