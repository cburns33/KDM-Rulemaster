# Combined Grab preview, 7 October 2026

Question: Maul targets the victim of Grab from the last round. What does Grab do, and what remains unverified for a physical 1.5 Maul ruling?

The **image-checked Grasp card** says that, after damage, its target suffers **Grab**: the target is placed knocked down in front of the monster and takes **1 damage per monster level**. The checked rulebook scan says this Grab damage is outside the attack profile, so monster damage attributes and damage tokens do not modify it, and it cannot be dodged.

Separately, the **unreviewed Maul wiki draft** lists “victim of Grab last round” as a target choice. The supplied sources do not verify that this draft matches a physical 1.5 Maul card, or establish that the checked Grasp card’s Grab is the Grab referenced by physical 1.5 Maul. That relationship remains unverified for a physical 1.5 ruling.

Sources: unreviewed Maul wiki revision 28126; image-checked Grasp card; revised rulebook pages 30, 47, and 48. This preview does not certify the physical 1.5 Maul card.
