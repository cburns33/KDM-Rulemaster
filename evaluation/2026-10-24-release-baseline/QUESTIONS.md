# First-release baseline questions, version 1

Authored 24 October 2026 after reviewing the current coverage inventory and prior project context, before running these questions or making answer-process changes for them. These are new scenario/wording cases, source-guided and authored with prior exposure to the project. They are not an independent blind benchmark.

Freeze this version for the first run. Do not edit a prompt after seeing its answer; append a dated revision separately. There are 32 scenarios and 36 user turns. Four scenarios have two turns. Each scenario starts a new conversation; preserve the actual first response's context only within that scenario. Do not preselect a record or table unless a question itself asks for it.

Submit only the question text below to the app, without headings, case IDs, reviewer criteria, or the release checklist. The [reviewer guide](REVIEWER_GUIDE.md) is separate. Cunning has no case in this set. No questions have been run yet.

## Starting the fight and taking turns

### Q01

We just named our four starting survivors and put their two starting cards on their grids. Do we each begin with some survival, and can I spend it to Encourage someone during this opening fight?

### Q02

We're setting up our first-ever lion encounter. Which cards should be guaranteed to appear first in the AI and hit-location decks, and should the rest of each deck be shuffled?

### Q03

I spend my activation to attack before moving. Once that attack has finished, can I take my movement, or did attacking use up my whole act?

### Q04

My named starter has a Founding Stone, no strength modifiers, and is next to the lion. For a normal attack, what do I roll to hit, and what gets added when I try to wound? Is that the same procedure as slinging it?

### Q05

We just wounded the lion and removed the final AI card from its discard pile. There are now no AI cards left anywhere except the wound stack. Is the lion dead now, or does something else have to happen?

### Q06

We won the opening fight, but one survivor died carrying gear and a resource gained during the fight. What happens to those items, and what rewards and Hunt XP do the living survivors receive?

## Card instructions and interactions

### Q07

Claw finds two equally close standing threats in the area the lion faces, both within movement range. Who chooses between them? Can that choice skip both and select a farther survivor instead?

### Q08

Power Swat scores its one hit, and I Dodge that hit before resolving damage. Should I still move my survivor six spaces? Which part of the card decides?

### Q09

Size Up rolls a 6 against a standing survivor with 2 insanity during a level 1 fight. What changes on that survivor's record, and do they remain standing?

### Q10

The survivor grabbed last round has since stood up. Another survivor is lying down closer to the lion. Maul is drawn. Does the first survivor still take priority, and does being picked make Grab happen again?

### Q11

Our level 1 lion has the +1 damage token from the opening Enraged example. Maul now damages its target. How does that token affect Maul, and is there also Grab damage because Maul mentions the previous Grab victim?

### Q12

Strange Hand's Lost Hand is in play when Combo Claw comes up. Of the adjacent survivors, one already received the Lost Hand understanding benefit earlier in their life and one has never received it. What should we resolve, and do we make attack rolls?

### Q13

Ground Fighting is in play. I use movement to pass through its colored zone, leave the zone, and then spend my activation outside it. Did passing through the zone trigger its attack? Please show the diagram that matters.

### Q14

I critically wound Strange Hand with zero survival left. Do I gain strength, and where should the hit-location card go after this attack?

### Q15

Two survivors are resolving attacks at the same time because of an effect. One draws Clever Ploy while the lion is knocked down, and the other still has an unresolved hit location. What happens to the lion and both attacks? When can the trapped attacker spend survival again?

### Q16

Claw has finished and the next AI card is Chomp. Please walk us through Chomp's target list, attack numbers, and any additional instructions using its own card.

### Q17

The next card in our opening fight is Terrifying Roar. What do we resolve, in what order, and which printed source are you using for those instructions?

### Q18

Enraged has just been drawn. Beyond the damage-token example in the tutorial, what happens when the mood enters play, what does it change while it stays out, and what makes us discard it?

## Follow-ups and changing setup

### Q19

Turn 1: We're resolving a lion AI card and nobody meets its target conditions. Does the lion still do the attack printed below them?

Turn 2: The card is Power Swat. Nobody matches either of its target conditions.

### Q20

Turn 1: For our opening Prologue fight, how far away do the survivors start, and do we use the normal random level 1 AI setup?

Turn 2: Now we mean the next hunt, a standard level 1 White Lion, not the Prologue. Should we copy that same deployment and fixed deck?

## Settlement and hunt

### Q21

We have finished the Prologue and are sitting down for our first settlement phase. In what order do we handle returning survivors, First Day, endeavors, and the lantern-year timeline?

### Q22

Do the four people who fought the Prologue determine our entire starting population, or does First Day establish that? Walk us through the actual First Day instructions before we record the settlement.

### Q23

We have reached our first settlement and are looking at Language. What does that innovation give the settlement, and which survival actions are available afterward? Please identify the source for each unlock.

### Q24

At our first settlement, we have resources in storage and a location card with a gear recipe. Walk us through how to check whether we can craft it, pay for it, and put the gear on a survivor. What additional card text would you need to tell us the recipe's exact cost?

### Q25

We rolled 84 on the hunt-event table. Tell us the exact event and all of its results. We haven't supplied the event text or a photograph.

### Q26

The standard lion setup gives us Tall Grass. How many tiles does that card set up, and what must a survivor do to gain its hiding benefit? Can you show both the setup evidence and the terrain effect?

### Q27

We're leaving the settlement to hunt a level 1 White Lion for the first time. How do we prepare the hunt board and advance through movement and events until the showdown starts?

### Q28

A normal hunt event causes 2 body damage to a survivor with zero body armor and both body injury boxes already checked. The event does not mention severe injuries. Do we roll severe injuries now, and do those existing checked boxes carry into the showdown?

## Tables, state, and edition boundaries

### Q29

This survivor reached their first heavy-bordered Hunt XP box and rolled a total of 13 on Age. What do they receive, and does choosing a weapon type give them a proficiency level right now?

### Q30

Turn 1: We reached Hands of Heat, do not have Lantern Oven, and rolled a final 6. Which branch and result do we resolve?

Turn 2: This is a separate later occurrence: we already have Lantern Oven before starting the event, and the final roll is 6 again. Do we use the same branch?

### Q31

Turn 1: A survivor rolled 8 for a severe Arms injury. What is the result?

Turn 2: I checked the die again: the damaged location was Head instead. The severe injury roll is still 8.

### Q32

We are switching this game to edition 1.6. Can we use your opening-fight card instructions as verified 1.6 rules, or which compatibility checks are still missing?
